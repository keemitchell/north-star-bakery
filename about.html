About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery focused on fresh, handmade baked goods. We want to provide a welcoming place for local customers, families, and visitors.

Our bakery started with a love for baking and sharing food with others. Today, we continue that tradition by making breads, pastries, and cakes for our community.

How We Bake

We focus on fresh ingredients and making our baked goods in small batches. When possible, we also work with local suppliers.
